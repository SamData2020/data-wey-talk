1. Who we are

Data Wey Talk is a free data-analytics education initiative run by Scitylana as part of its corporate social responsibility work. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Scitylana. We are responsible for the personal information collected through this website.

Contact: scitylanaltd@gmail.com

3. What we do NOT collect

  • Files you analyse. CSV files you open in the analysis tool are read inside your browser and are never uploaded to us.
  • My Book. Jobs, expenses, clients, prices, receipts and report cards you create in My Book are stored only on your device. We can't see them unless you choose to send them to us.
  • Advertising or tracking. We don't use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics trackers, and we don't sell or rent your personal information.

4. Information stored on your device

To make the site work, we save some information in your browser's storage on your device (not cookies). It stays on that device and is not sent to us, except where section 2 says so:

  • your language choice, and that you completed the sign-up form, with your name, email, phone and country to fill in the quiz and game for you;
  • your quiz progress and the month you took it, your game numbers, and your My Book records;
  • a copy of the website's files so it can open without internet.

You can delete all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. If others use the same phone or browser, they may be able to see it.

5. Who we share information with

We only share information with service providers that help us run the site:

  • Google (Google Sheets, Apps Script and Gmail) stores the information from section 2 and sends us prize notifications.
  • GitHub hosts the website. Like any web host, its servers may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit.
  • Google Fonts provides the website's fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when the page loads.
  • WhatsApp, your email app or your phone's share menu, only when you choose to share a receipt, report card, reminder or link.
  • Gift-card or voucher providers, only for the monthly winner, and only the details needed to send the prize.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We never sell your information.

6. International transfers

Our service providers may store information on servers outside your country, including in the United States and Europe. They use recognised safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Sign-up and game information: up to 24 months after your last activity with us, or until you ask us to delete it or withdraw your consent.
  • Quiz entries and prize records: up to 24 months after the month of the quiz, so we can show the prize was run fairly.
  • Emails: as long as needed to deal with your request.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the UK GDPR, Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, South Africa's POPIA or the EU GDPR), you can:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it or delete it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this doesn't affect anything we did before);
  • object to or ask us to limit how we use it;
  • complain to your data-protection authority, such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), or the authority in your country.

To use any of these rights, email scitylanaltd@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

9. Security

Only authorised Scitylana staff can open the spreadsheets where your information is stored, and those accounts are protected. The website can add entries but cannot read stored entries back. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you and the authorities if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

10. Children

Data Wey Talk is meant for adults and young people learning about business. It is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us their details, contact us and we will delete them.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes, we will also show a notice on the website.
