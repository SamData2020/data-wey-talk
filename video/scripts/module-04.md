## Slide 1 — Di numbers wey matter

Welcome to Module 4. Today we go talk about di numbers wey really matter for your business.

## Slide 2 — Sales no be profit

Plenty people dey confuse sales with profit. If you sell one hundred thousand naira, but you spend seventy thousand, your profit na only thirty thousand.

## Slide 3 — Profit margin

Profit margin tell you how much you dey keep from every one hundred naira. Thirty percent mean say from every hundred naira, thirty naira na your own.

## Slide 4 — Other numbers to watch

Watch your average sale, which product dey carry di biggest share, and how dis month take compare with last month.

## Slide 5 — How e go help you

With dis numbers, when you increase price, you go see whether profit go up, or customers don begin run.
