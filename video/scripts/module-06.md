Welcome to Module 6. Today we go find di pattern wey dey hide inside your data.

## Slide 2 — Your best days and time

Check which day and which time dey busy pass. For akara seller, Saturday morning fit be di best time. Plan for am.

## Slide 3 — Fast movers and slow movers

Some goods dey finish quick, some dey sit down for shelf. Di ones wey dey sit down dey hold your money. Buy more of di fast ones.

## Slide 4 — Seasons

Season dey matter. Festive period, school resumption, rainy season — each one dey change how people dey buy. Compare your locations too.

## Slide 5 — How e go help you

When you sabi di pattern, you go prepare before di busy time reach, instead of to dey regret after.
