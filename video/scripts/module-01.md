## Slide 1 — Data na wetin? Start to dey write your records

How you dey! Welcome to Scitylana Academy. Today, we go talk about data — wetin e be, and how you go take start to dey write your records.

## Slide 2 — Everything wey happen for your business na data

Data no be big grammar. Every time you sell something, na data. Every money wey you spend, na data. Every customer wey buy from you, na data.

## Slide 3 — Where you go write am?

You fit write am for notebook, for your phone, or for Excel. Any one wey you choose, di important thing na say make you dey write am every day.

## Slide 4 — Simple daily record

See am like dis. Date, wetin you sell, how many, and di amount. Your expense too — like di money for fuel or oil — write am, put minus for front.

## Slide 5 — How e go help you

When you don write for two or three weeks, you go begin see pattern. You go know which day market dey move, and which day e dey slow. Na dis one go help you plan.
